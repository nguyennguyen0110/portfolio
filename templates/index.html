Who I am

About me:

I'm Nguyen The Anh Nguyen, or Nguyen Nguyen for short. You can also call me Martin. I was born in 1987. I'm living in Dong Nai city, Vietnam.

My professional journey began in the printing industry, where I worked as both a graphic designer and later a production manager. Over more than a decade, I built expertise in production planning, quality control, and collaborating with international partners to implement new technologies. These experiences developed my attention to detail, problem-solving skills, and leadership abilities.

After being affected by the COVID-19 pandemic, I decided to pivot toward software development — a field that was growing rapidly at the time. I initially taught myself programming, which sparked a deep interest in software development. I later formalized my learning through a "Full Stack Java Web Developer" course at FPT Academy and a "Data Science" program at Funix. Since then I have worked as a backend and full-stack developer with Python (Flask, Django), Java (Spring Boot), and TypeScript (Bun/Hono, Next.js). For my own projects, such as Romi, I use Rust.

Ten years on a print shop floor taught me to plan, to find the real cause of a defect, and to finish what I start. I write software the same way.

Career direction:

I work full-time in software development, and that is where I want to keep growing. I am open to the right opportunity, in software or in a production-related role.
